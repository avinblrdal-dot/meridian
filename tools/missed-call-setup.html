Missed-call setup (Path B)

Path B means Meridian turns on the missed-call texts, auto-replies and reminders that a salon's existing booking app or phone service already includes, instead of building a new texting system. It costs $0 extra because nothing new is bought: no Twilio number, no new subscription, and no carrier registration on Meridian's side. The catch is that a true automatic text after a missed call is built into only a few tools, so for most salons Path B closes most of the gap, not all of it.

Start here: ask the owner which tools they use

The answers tell you which sections of this page apply. Ask before the setup visit, by phone or on a quick drop-in.

  1. What do clients book through, and which plan are you on? If they don't know the plan, open the billing or subscription page in the app together. Plan decides what's included.
  2. What number do clients call? Your own cell (iPhone or Android), a landline, or a business phone app like Grasshopper, Quo (OpenPhone), RingCentral, Dialpad or Google Voice? Which carrier? A business phone app is the most likely place a real missed-call text already exists.
  3. When you're with a client and the phone rings, what happens now? Voicemail, rings out, someone else picks up?
  4. Can clients already text that number? Does anyone read and answer those texts?
  5. Do you have Instagram and Facebook business pages, and can you log in to them here?
  6. Do you manage your Google Business Profile (the listing on Google Maps)?
  7. Do you send promotions or specials by text today? How did clients agree to get them? Path B covers replies and reminders only. Marketing texts need written consent (see Consent basics).
Quick routing from the answers
  • Business phone app (Quo, Grasshopper, RingCentral): a real missed-call text may already be in their plan. Start with that section.
  • Mindbody on the Ultimate plan, or Zenoti with HyperConnect: the booking tool itself can text missed callers. Start there.
  • Personal cell plus Square, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Booksy, Fresha, Boulevard or Acuity: no built-in automatic missed-call text was found for these. Do the "close the gap" setup: reminders, auto-replies, one-tap Respond with Text, voicemail greeting, Google booking link, Instagram and Facebook replies.
  • They say "Schedulicity": Schedulicity is now Vagaro. Use the Vagaro section.

Booking tools: what to turn on

Menus change. Follow the path, then match the label on screen. Steps marked * come from official pages checked through search excerpts, so open the source before the visit.

Square Appointments

ConfirmedMissed-call text: none foundAuto-reply: Square AssistantReminders: yes

Turn on Square Assistant (answers client texts)

  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard. Go to Appointments > Settings > Communications.
  2. Select Square Assistant, toggle it on, click Save.
  3. Other route: open the Messages inbox, click the gear icon, toggle Enable Square Assistant, Save.

What it does, per Square: sends SMS appointment reminders, and clients can confirm, reschedule or cancel by text. The calendar updates on its own. Square lists it for Appointments Free, Plus and Premium. The person setting it up needs the "customers" permission.

Turn on text reminders

  1. Appointments > Settings > Communications.
  2. In Confirmations and Reminders, toggle on the text reminder (and email if wanted).
  3. Pick how far ahead from the drop-down. Click Save.

Check: Square's reminders article says it applies to Appointments Plus and Premium, and to Free, Plus or Premium sellers with advanced booking added. Confirm on the owner's plan page. Forum posts (Third-party) report Square Assistant sometimes answered a client with the wrong policy, so text it yourself and read its replies for the first week. Don't turn on "Do Not Disturb" in Messages: per Square's help*, it sends a fixed reply telling clients messaging isn't active.

Vagaro

Confirmed*Missed-call text: none foundAuto-reply: paid add-onReminders: free

Turn on reminders*

  1. Phone: More > Settings > Booking > Email & Text Notifications. Web or tablet: Settings gear > Booking > Email & Text Notifications.
  2. Turn on Appointment Reminder and choose how far ahead it sends.
  3. For one client: open their profile, Edit, and under Appointment Reminders make sure SMS Text is on.

Vagaro says standard reminders are available to all Vagaro businesses at no charge. They come from Vagaro's short code 89885, and replies to those automated texts do not reach the business. A client who texted STOP can text START to 89885. Standard reminder wording can't be edited.

What costs extra

Texting clients by SMS from Connect (Vagaro's inbox) needs a Text Messaging plan. The Vagaro AI chatbot can auto-reply 24/7 to chats from the Vagaro listing page and, with a text plan, to SMS. Vagaro's help says turning it on adds a monthly subscription. Not Path B unless the owner already pays for it.

GlossGenius

ConfirmedMissed-call text: not describedAuto-reply: Reception add-onReminders: yes

Reminders

GlossGenius sends automated text reminders and confirmations and says client notifications come "at no extra cost." They go out even to clients who haven't opted in to marketing. Text wording can't be customized; extra language can only be added to emails.

  1. Web: Settings > Business > Client Notifications, then Reminders. App: Features > Settings > Client Notifications.*
  2. Check timing. One help article shows a different path (Settings > Booking Controls & Notifications), so match what's on screen.

An AI front desk that answers calls and texts 24/7 and books into the calendar. It sits under Features > Reception and works with call forwarding. GlossGenius's page says it is free until Nov 30, 2026, then $50/month (100 minutes and 200 texts, with overage charges). Its pages don't describe a text after a missed call.

Only turn Reception on if the owner understands it becomes paid and agrees to decide before Nov 30. Write the date down for them.

Fresha

ConfirmedMissed-call text: not describedAuto-reply: AI Concierge add-onReminders: yes

Set up reminders

  1. Desktop: Marketing > Automations (left panel). Phone: More > Marketing > Messaging > Automations.
  2. Open a reminder, then Options > Edit. Set timing under Trigger.
  3. Pick channels: email, text, WhatsApp. "Or" tries WhatsApp first and falls back to text.

Fresha says email reminders are always free; text and WhatsApp reminders come out of the messaging balance (a monthly allowance, then paid; see pricing). Up to three reminders per appointment.

Client Connect (clients message the business from their booking in Fresha) is included with every plan. AI Concierge is a separate add-on that answers calls and can auto-reply to messages; the business can forward its existing number to it. Fresha doesn't describe a text after a missed call.

Mindbody

Confirmed*Missed-call text: yes (AI Concierge)Reminders: yes, opt-in

Mindbody's AI Concierge (older name: Messenger[ai]) follows up on missed calls by text. Mindbody says AI Concierge is included in the Ultimate plan; for other plans, ask Mindbody. Basic Messenger does not include the missed-call text.

Turn on the missed-call text*

  1. In AI Concierge, find the call forwarding number under Phone Settings.
  2. Ask the phone company to forward unanswered calls to that number. Do not give the carrier the AI Concierge texting number.
  3. Record a greeting that tells callers they'll get a text with booking options.
  4. Check Preferences > Missed Call Settings (it can respond to the voicemail's content and offer to rebook a past appointment).

Mindbody says the caller usually gets the text within about a minute, and a client gets at most one missed-call text per 24 hours. Pausing the bot pauses these texts.

Text reminders*

  1. Settings > General > General Setup and Options, expand System Settings, enable Automated Mobile Text Messages, click Update.
  2. New client profiles default to email only. Switch them to "Email and Text" under Client Info > Contact & Subscriptions, or clients can text START to the studio's number.

Mindbody recommends using either Messenger[ai] confirmations or standard reminders, not both, to avoid duplicate texts.

Phones and messaging: what to turn on

iPhone

Confirmed*Missed-call text: noOne-tap reply: yes

Best $0 option: Respond with Text (manual, one tap)

  1. Settings > Apps > Phone > Respond with Text.
  2. Tap one of the fields under Respond With and type the booking-link message (see templates).
  3. When a call comes in during a service: tap Message and pick that reply. The call goes to voicemail and the caller gets the text.

Not recommended: Driving Focus auto-reply

Settings > Focus > Driving > Auto-Reply (No One, Recents, Favorites, All Contacts). Apple describes it as replying to people who message you, not to callers, and it silences notifications. It's built for driving. Whether a caller gets it is Not confirmed.

Also update the voicemail greeting so it names the booking link (script in the setup visit section).

ConfirmedSteps: RingCentral communityMissed-call text: build it

RingCentral's Workflow Builder has a Missed call trigger and a Send SMS action. There's no simple on/off setting; a built-in "auto reply SMS to missed calls" request has been marked Planned since April 2024.

What Meridian does in a setup visit

Plan for about an hour. The owner stays in control of every account; Meridian just knows where the switches are.

  1. Before: ask the start-here questions. Read the matching sections and open their sources that day, since menus change.
  2. Logins: the owner signs in on their own device. Never ask for, type in, or write down their passwords.
  3. No new charges: don't turn on anything that adds a fee. If a feature is a paid add-on or a free period that turns paid, show the owner the pricing page and let them decide.
  4. Booking link: copy it from their account and open it on your phone. Check services and times look right.
  5. Reminders: turn on text reminders and confirmations in the booking tool. Pick the timing with the owner.
  6. Auto-replies: turn on whatever their tools include (Square Assistant, Boulevard after-hours, Quo or Grasshopper missed-call reply, and so on).
  7. Personal cell: set Respond with Text or Quick responses with the booking-link message, and update the voicemail greeting.
  8. Online: add the booking link to Google Business Profile; set the Instagram/Facebook instant reply and FAQs.
  9. Test everything: call from your phone and let it ring out, text the number, message on Instagram. Check what arrives, how fast, and that the link opens. Turn off anything that misfires.
  10. Leave a summary: a short list of what was turned on, where it lives, how to turn it off, and any dates that matter (GlossGenius Reception turns paid after Nov 30, 2026; Boulevard AI Receptionist billing starts Dec 1, 2026).
  11. Follow up in 1 to 2 weeks: ask whether clients mentioned the texts, check for wrong automated answers, and log the result.

Missed-call text (Quo, Grasshopper, etc.)

Keep it under 160 characters including the link. Business name first, no offers.

[Salon]: sorry we missed your call, we're with a client. Book here: [link] or reply and we'll text back soon.

Respond with Text / Quick response

Sent by the owner with one tap while the phone rings.

With a client right now. Book here: [link] or I'll call you back soon. - [Name], [Salon]

Instagram / Facebook instant reply

Thanks for messaging [Salon]! The fastest way to book is here: [link]. Questions? Leave them here and we'll answer between clients.

Voicemail greeting

Links can't be clicked in a greeting, so say where to find it.

You've reached [Salon]. We're probably with a client. You can book anytime online: search [Salon] on Google or Instagram and tap Book. Or leave a message and we'll call you back today.

Consent basics (not legal advice)

Path B stays on the safe side by sending only replies and reminders. When in doubt, don't send it, and tell the owner to ask someone qualified.

  • One reply to someone who just called or messaged is generally treated as answering the customer, not marketing. Industry guidelines (CTIA) call a quick, single reply to a consumer's text "conversational." A text after a call is close to that, but some providers are cautious: RingCentral removed its missed-call templates over consent risk. So: one message, name the business, no promotions.
  • Appointment reminders are informational. Clients agree to them when they book, and the booking tools handle opt-in (Acuity and Mindbody ask clients directly). Don't copy client lists into another tool to text them.
  • Promotions ("20% off this week", "time to rebook") are marketing. The FCC's consumer guide says commercial texts need written consent. Texas also extended its telephone solicitation law to cover marketing texts starting Sept 1, 2025. Path B setups should not include marketing texts.
  • STOP always works. The booking tools add opt-out handling automatically. Don't remove opt-out wording or re-add someone who opted out.
  • Carrier registration applies to any business texting number (Quo, Grasshopper, RingCentral). The provider walks the owner through it; it's the business's registration, not Meridian's.

Sources: FCC: Stop unwanted robocalls and texts* · CTIA Messaging Principles and Best Practices (2023, PDF)* · Morgan Lewis: Texas law now covers text messages*

When Path B isn't enough

Be upfront with the owner about these gaps. Don't oversell what was set up.

  • Their number is a personal cell. Neither iPhone nor Android documents an automatic missed-call text. Respond with Text still needs someone to tap.
  • The feature exists but costs extra. GlossGenius Reception (after Nov 30, 2026), Boulevard AI Receptionist (from Dec 1, 2026), Fresha AI Concierge, Vagaro AI, Mindbody AI Concierge outside Ultimate, Verizon Business Assistant. Then it's the owner's decision, not $0.
  • "Free" features that bill per text. Fresha (after the monthly allowance), Boulevard (usage rates), Zenoti (SMS credits).
  • Replies go nowhere. Reminder texts from shared numbers (Vagaro 89885, Acuity's assigned number, Booksy blasts) don't deliver client replies to the owner.
  • Only some callers get a text. Grasshopper's default is first-time callers only; Mindbody sends at most one per 24 hours; Quo at most one per 3 hours.
The other option, pending a decision: Meridian-run texting through Twilio, sending texts from a number Meridian sets up. It needs US carrier A2P 10DLC registration and has monthly and per-message costs. Avin hasn't decided on it yet. Until he does, don't offer it, quote a price, or promise it. If an owner asks, say: That's something I'm still looking into. For now I can set up what your current tools already do.
