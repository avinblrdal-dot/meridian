Your assistant, ready to try.

This is a stand-in for your website. The part that matters is the chat bubble in the corner - that's exactly what your visitors would get.

Try to catch it out

  • Ask about your hours, your prices, parking, cancellations.
  • Type it badly on purpose. Misspell things. It's built for that.
  • Ask it something it can't possibly know.

That last one is the important test. It says "I'm not sure - give us a call" rather than guessing. It can only ever tell your customers things you've told it, which means it can't quote a wrong price.
