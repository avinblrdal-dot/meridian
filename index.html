How it works

You don't get a login and a help article. We set it up in your shop.

Audit

We sit down with you and look at where bookings slip: missed calls, no-shows, messages, and reviews. It's free.

Setup

We install the tool ourselves, including the call forwarding, and write the text in your voice.

Training

We show you and your front desk how to run it and stay until everyone is comfortable.

Follow-up

Once it has been running for a while, we compare missed calls and no-shows against your numbers from before.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

The audit is free. If it turns up something worth fixing, we quote the setup once, based on what it involves. A small monthly plan to keep things running is optional. We're taking on a few founding clients at a reduced rate in exchange for sharing their before-and-after results.

Do I have to switch booking software?

No. The text includes a link to whatever you already use to take bookings.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your clients keep calling the number they already have. We set up the forwarding so unanswered calls trigger the text.

What if a caller doesn't want texts?

They can reply STOP and they won't get another one. See our privacy page for what we keep.

Can I change what the text says?

Yes. You choose the wording, and we'll help you write it so it sounds like your shop.
